Ancient Ruins

Project
Reconstructed Memory
Year
2025
Category
Archival, Architecture, Animation, Generative ai
Synopsis

Ancient Ruins explores the intersection of history, art, and technology. Starting with public domain sketches of Egyptian ruins, I used generative AI tools to transform these antique drawings into photorealistic, cinematic scenes, imagining what these ancient worlds might look like through a modern lens. Through AI-powered video generation, the still images were brought to life with sweeping camera movements and atmospheric motion, reanimating fragments of the past using the cutting edge of digital creativity.

Stage 1
Image Reconstruction

This phase focused on reinterpreting archival imagery through the lens of generative AI. Each historical sketch was processed with prompts designed to “photograph” the past turning ancient lines and textures into cinematic landscapes rendered with striking realism.

Stage 2
Color Matching

The phase, all images are carefully color matched to one another to give a cohesive look and feel which will help in the next phase of geneateing animations from each image
