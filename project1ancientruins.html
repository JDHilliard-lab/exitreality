Project
Ancient Ruins
Year
2025
Category
Archival, Architecture, Animation, Generative AI
Synopsis

Ancient Ruins reimagines historical sketches of Egyptian sites through generative AI. Public domain drawings were transformed into photorealistic, cinematic scenes and animated with AI-driven video generation, blending art, history, and technology to breathe new life into the ancient past.

Stage 1
Image Reconstruction

This phase focused on reinterpreting archival imagery through the lens of generative AI. Each historical sketch was processed with prompts designed to "photograph" the past, turning ancient lines and textures into cinematic landscapes rendered with striking realism.
