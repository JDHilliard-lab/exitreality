Project
Ocean Oddities
Year
2025
Category
Creature design, Animation, Generative AI
Synopsis

This project imagines aquatic life on distant worlds through traditional and digital techniques. Watercolor paintings of fictional fish were transformed with generative AI into photorealistic images, converted into 3D models, and composed in Blender. Final stills were animated with image-to-video generation, bringing these imagined extraterrestrial creatures to life.

Process
Image Transformationn

Hand-painted watercolor fish were transformed into photorealistic underwater creatures using generative AI.

Stage 2
Image to 3D

In this phase, the photorealistic versions are placed on a clean white background for easy processing from image to 3D generative assets

Stage 3
Animation & Motion

The final stage brings these static images to life through AI-powered video generation. Each rendered scene receives dynamic camera movements and atmospheric effects, creating immersive cinematic sequences that explore these reconstructed ancient worlds.
